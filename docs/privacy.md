# LyricPlayer privacy

LyricPlayer runs Whisper on your iPhone.
When you choose a file, the app saves a local copy of the audio and a timestamped transcript in its Documents folder.
The app has no sign-in, tracking SDK, analytics service, or server to receive your audio or transcript.
Your device backup settings and any file provider you use are managed separately by you.

The optional Music tab requests permission to read the current track from your media library and match its title to a local LRC file.
You can use the Recordings tab without granting Music access.

You can remove an import by deleting its folder in Files under **On My iPhone → LyricPlayer → Recordings**.
Deleting the app removes its local documents.
For privacy questions, open a [project issue](https://github.com/rahul-nath/LyricPlayer/issues) without attaching private recordings or transcripts.
